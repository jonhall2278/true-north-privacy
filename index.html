True North Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

This policy covers the True North app for iPhone and the True North service at tnbrain.com ("True North"). True North is provided by HFB Technologies, 4617 Pioneer Rd Suite 200, St. George, Utah 84790 ("we", "us"). It's for businesses that already have a True North account, set up with HFB Technologies or one of its partner agencies. Accounts are not created in the app.

Information True North collects

  • Account information: your name, email address, the business you belong to and your role there. Passwords are stored only in a scrambled (hashed) form.
  • What you put into True North: your chat messages, the text of what you say when you use voice, photos you take or choose, files you upload, and what True North makes for your business (for example website pages, images and documents).
  • Your business's connected tools: which tools your business has connected to True North. The sign-in details for those tools are kept encrypted by True North's connector service and are never shown to the AI.
  • Technical information needed to run and protect the service, such as sign-in sessions, IP addresses and error logs.

The app asks for the microphone, speech recognition, camera and photos only when you first use a feature that needs them, and saying no turns off only that feature. When you use voice, Apple's speech recognition turns your speech into text, under Apple's privacy policy.

How we use it

Only to provide True North to you and your business: to answer your requests, make and change what you ask for, keep your history, and support and secure the service. We do not sell your information, we do not use it for advertising, and the app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

AI providers

To answer you, True North sends your messages, voice text, photos and files, along with the business information they need, to these AI providers. They process it to produce True North's answers:

  • Anthropic (Claude): chats and the work True North does for you. Anthropic's privacy policy
  • OpenAI (Codex): some building and coding work. OpenAI's privacy policy
  • Higgsfield: images and video. Higgsfield's privacy policy

The app asks for your permission before anything is sent, the first time you open it.

Other service providers

True North runs on DigitalOcean (servers and databases), uses GitHub and Vercel to store and host the websites and apps it builds for your business, and uses Postmark to send email. They handle information only to provide those services to us.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), access to it is limited to the people and systems that need it, and the sign-in details for your connected tools are stored encrypted.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your business's True North account is active. To delete your account and its data, email support@hfbtechnologies.com from the email address on the account. We delete it within 30 days, except anything the law requires us to keep. Your business's account administrator can also remove users from the business's account.

Children

True North is a tool for businesses and is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll also let your business know by email or in True North.
