True North Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

This policy covers the True North iPhone app and the True North service at tnbrain.com (“True North”). True North is provided by HFB Technologies, 4617 Pioneer Rd Suite 200, St. George, Utah 84790 (“we”, “us”). True North is for businesses and agencies with an existing account arranged through HFB Technologies or a partner agency. Accounts are not created in the iPhone app.

Information we collect and how we collect it

  • Account and business information: your account identifier, name, email address, business or agency, and account role, supplied when your account is arranged or maintained.
  • Content you provide: messages and instructions, uploaded files, photos, video and audio, and speech converted to text when you use voice. We receive this information when you enter, record, select or upload it through True North.
  • Workspace information: conversation history, saved business knowledge in Brain, relevant business details, task instructions, and generated answers, code, websites, documents and media. True North stores this information as you and your business use the service.
  • Connected services: connection details and information your business authorizes True North to retrieve from connected tools. Retrieved information can become part of a task or the context sent to an AI service.
  • Service and security information: sign-in/session information, IP addresses and operational or error records generated when the service is used.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, apply business access permissions, answer requests, perform tasks, create and edit content, retrieve relevant business knowledge, preserve work and conversation history, connect authorized services, provide support, and operate and protect True North. Your instructions and relevant saved context may be included together in an AI request; processing is not limited to the most recent message you type. Messages, files and retrieved business context may contain personal, contact or financial information about you, your customers or others.

AI services and information shared

True North uses the services below. Which service receives information depends on the task and the services connected for your business. This does not mean every provider receives every message or every file.

  • Anthropic (Claude): messages, instructions, relevant conversation history, business and Brain context, and relevant uploaded content for chat, analysis and business tasks. See Anthropic’s privacy information and Claude Code legal and compliance information.
  • OpenAI (Codex): coding/building instructions, relevant code or project files and task context when the connected coding service is used. See OpenAI’s privacy policy and Codex authentication and data-handling information.
  • Higgsfield: prompts, reference images, video or audio and related instructions when generating or editing media. Higgsfield may share relevant inputs with third-party AI model and compute providers to deliver those features. Higgsfield’s general terms permit using submitted content and generated results to train and improve its and its affiliates’ models. Separate Enterprise Agreements can provide different protections; we do not represent that those agreements apply to all True North usage. See Higgsfield’s privacy policy and terms.
  • Descript: audio or video, transcripts, and editing instructions when using its media editing and transcription features. See Descript’s privacy policy.
  • Google (Gemini), when connected: the instructions, relevant content and task context needed for a request using that connected service. Google’s treatment of Gemini API content differs between paid and unpaid services, including whether content may be used to improve its products. See Gemini API terms and Google’s privacy policy.

AI services may retain submitted information and results under their applicable terms, settings and legal obligations. We do not promise that all AI processing has zero retention or that all providers exclude content from model training.

Voice and device permissions

Voice input may use speech recognition supplied by your device or browser to convert speech into text. Processing depends on that implementation and may involve its service provider; we do not promise that speech recognition stays on your device. Transcribed text can be included in an AI request. Spoken replies can be generated by True North’s self-hosted text-to-speech service. Audio or video submitted for a media task can also be processed by the services identified above.

When a feature needs microphone, camera or photo access, the device may request the applicable permission. You can manage device permissions in system settings. Giving device access and agreeing to third-party AI processing are separate choices.

Your AI choice in the iPhone app

Before opening the workspace, the iPhone app explains AI sharing and asks you to agree. Choosing “Not now” keeps the workspace closed. You can read the explanation again from the app’s AI privacy control. Reading it or opening this policy does not change your agreement.

You can withdraw agreement from that control. Withdrawal closes the workspace in this app and stops future AI use through it until you agree again. It does not delete information already retained, close your business account, revoke connected-service authorizations, cancel previously submitted work or automations running on servers, or change consent on another device. Contact us and your business administrator for help with those requests.

Other recipients and business services

Your business’s authorized users and administrators may have access according to their roles. True North uses DigitalOcean for hosting and storage, GitHub and Vercel for development and hosting of work it builds, and Postmark for service email. The information involved depends on the service being delivered. When you authorize a connected tool or ask True North to publish, send or otherwise act on information, that information is also shared with the destination needed for that action.

Business telephone features outside the iPhone app may use Twilio to handle calls and related communications. Those features are distinct from device/browser voice input in the app. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to address fraud, abuse, security incidents or legal claims.

Security

The iPhone workspace uses HTTPS connections. We work to protect information against unauthorized access, use and disclosure. No online service can guarantee complete security. This policy does not claim that every AI provider has identical retention, training or account settings.

Retention and deletion

We retain account and workspace information while it is needed to provide your business’s service, preserve its work and history, support the service, and meet legal or security obligations. AI and connected-service providers may keep information under their own applicable retention policies.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information or account, email support@hfbtechnologies.com from your account email address. We may need to verify the request and coordinate with your business administrator where information belongs to a shared business workspace. We delete your account and its data within 30 days of a verified deletion request, except information the law requires us to retain. This is our commitment for information we control; independent providers' retention periods, backup exceptions and legal obligations may differ. We will explain applicable provider-specific limits and help with deletion requests to those providers. Deletion cannot undo prior incorporation of content into a trained AI model. Removing a user’s access does not by itself delete the business’s shared history or erase information already sent to another service.

Children

True North is a business tool and is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We update this page and its effective date when this policy changes. For significant changes, we will notify your business by email or in True North. The iPhone app may ask you to agree to an updated AI explanation before opening the workspace.
